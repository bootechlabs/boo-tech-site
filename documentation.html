FAQs

What format are the documents in?

Policies, procedures, and guides are delivered as Word (.docx) or PDF documents; workbooks and trackers are delivered as Excel (.xlsx) files.

Do I get future updates?

Per the License Agreement included with your purchase, Boo-Tech maintains each toolkit on an ongoing basis (refining language, correcting control mappings, and expanding coverage), and licensed customers can expect periodic updates delivered through the same channel as their original purchase.

Why do e1, i1, and r2 cover different numbers of domains?

Each tier's toolkit is scoped to match its certification pathway: e1 includes the 6 governance-foundation domains (01, 13, 14, 15, 17, 19); i1 includes 17 of 19 domains (adds 11 technical domains to e1's governance base); r2 includes all 19 domains and 172 controls, plus the Enterprise Artifacts suite. See each product page for the exact domain list and file count.
