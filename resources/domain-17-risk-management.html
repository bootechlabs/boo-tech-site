HITRUST Domain 17: Risk Management Policy Template

Domain 17 establishes the risk management framework the rest of the program runs on: how risks get identified and assessed, how treatment decisions get made, how the risk register is maintained, and how supply-chain risk factors in.

Why not just use a free generic template? Free HITRUST templates are a reasonable starting shape, and we point people to good ones on our Free Resources page. What they don't have is what this policy is built around: every one of its 40 control requirement statements carries a [BUID Reference] marker traceable to a specific HITRUST requirement, plus a HITRUST ↔ HIPAA ↔ NIST ↔ ISO 27001 crosswalk. Those are the two things that actually save time in front of an assessor.
