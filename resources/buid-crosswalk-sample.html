What [BUID Reference] tagging is

Every control requirement statement in a Boo-Tech policy carries a bracketed citation, for example [0101.00a1Organizational.123], tying that specific sentence back to a specific HITRUST CSF requirement statement. It's the difference between a policy that says "we review access periodically" and one an assessor can trace line-by-line to what they're actually testing.

What the crosswalk adds

Every Governance Accelerator also ships a Framework Crosswalk mapping those same requirement statements across HITRUST ↔ HIPAA ↔ NIST ↔ ISO 27001, so one control citation tells you where you stand against four frameworks at once instead of one.
